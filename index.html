About Swatch Studio

Swatch Studio is an interactive 3D colour wheel created by Kaustubh Jogle. Explore 50 digital colour swatches across seven palettes, lift a shade toward you, and copy its HEX or RGB value.

How do I use the colour wheel?

Drag left or right to rotate the wheel, or click a swatch to bring it to the six o’clock position and lift it. Focus the wheel and use the Left and Right arrow keys, or use the previous and next buttons. The selected colour panel shows its name, HEX, and RGB values with copy controls.

Can I browse colours automatically?

Use Play beside the reset button. The slideshow waits two seconds, then advances one colour every two seconds within the selected palette. Use Pause to stop; selecting a colour or dragging the wheel also pauses playback.

Are the screen colours exact physical matches?

No. This educational study uses lightly adjusted approximate screen colours based on the 2026 Pantone accompanying palettes. Permission is pending. It is not an authorized or certified colour reference, and cosmetic HEX changes do not establish permission. Screen settings and 3D lighting affect appearance. Use appropriate physical samples for print, filament, or manufacturing decisions.

Does Swatch Studio work on mobile?

Yes. The wheel fits desktop and mobile layouts. Its interactive 3D view requires JavaScript and WebGL. Light and dark themes and the separate black or white stand setting are saved locally in your browser. This guide is readable without JavaScript.
