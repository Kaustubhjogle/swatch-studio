## Platform

## Stack
Delegated by user for performance: React, Vite, Three.js with demand rendering.
## Users
People exploring colour through the physical metaphor of a filament swatch wheel.
## Product Purpose
Explore 50 colours across seven restored 2026 palettes for a local educational study in an interactive 3D wheel.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Drag to rotate; click to select; selected swatch lifts at six o'clock toward the viewer. Right panel shows a large colour sample, HEX, RGB and colour name with copy. Seasonal collections, saving and filament matching are out of scope. Use supplied stand and swatch STL geometry. Neutral off-white studio with restrained pale blue components is user pinned. Screen colours are approximate.
