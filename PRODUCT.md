# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated by user for performance: React, Vite, Three.js with demand rendering.
## Users
People exploring colour through the physical metaphor of a filament swatch wheel.
## Product Purpose
Explore Pantone's 2026 Colour of the Year and accompanying palettes in an interactive 3D wheel.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Drag to rotate; click to select; selected swatch lifts at six o'clock toward the viewer. Right panel shows a large colour sample, HEX, colour name and Pantone code with copy. Seasonal collections, saving and filament matching are out of scope. Use supplied stand and swatch STL geometry. Neutral off-white studio with restrained pale blue components is user pinned. Screen colours are approximate.
## Evidence on Hand
User-supplied Top, Bottom and Bambu Lab Style Filament Swatch STL files in Downloads. Official Pantone 2026 collection page identifies Cloud Dancer 11-4201 and seven palettes.
